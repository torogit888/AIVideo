Its design is sleek and futuristic, predominantly composed of polished metallic surfaces (titanium, aluminum alloys) in shades of silver, dark grey, and white, reflecting the deep space environment. Key features include a large, primary mirror aperture, possibly protected by a sunshield or baffle, and prominent, multi-panel solar arrays often depicted in deep blue or black, sometimes with golden Kapton insulation accents. The overall form factor should convey immense precision, robust engineering, and a sense of silent, powerful observation, distinct from the more compact, cylindrical form of Hubble, hinting at its 'hundreds of times' wider field of view through its overall scale and the complexity of its optical assembly. There are no visible human elements or organic textures; it is a purely technological marvel, a 'cosmic eye' designed for unparalleled cosmic vision.
> **環境錨定 (Environment Anchor):** The overarching environment is the profound, boundless expanse of deep space. This is characterized by an infinite, inky black vacuum, occasionally punctuated by the ethereal glow of distant nebulae and galaxies. Dominant colors are deep blues, purples, and blacks for the void, contrasted with vibrant, luminous hues from cosmic phenomena: swirling reds, oranges, and pinks of emission nebulae; cool blues of reflection nebulae; and the golden-white to blue-white light of countless stars. Distant galaxies appear as intricate spirals or elliptical blurs of light. Lighting is primarily natural cosmic illumination; stars are sharp, brilliant pinpricks of light, while nebulae provide soft, volumetric, and diffuse glows, creating gradients of color and light within the darkness. There are no harsh shadows in the traditional sense, but rather areas of profound darkness where no light sources are present, and gradual transitions where light from distant objects fades into the void. The overall effect is one of awe-inspiring grandeur, silent majesty, and infinite mystery, with textures ranging from the smooth emptiness of space to the wispy, gaseous, and often turbulent forms of nebulae and the granular sparkle of dense star fields.
